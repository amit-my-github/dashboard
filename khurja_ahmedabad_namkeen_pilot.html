Key question: “If we spend ₹10,000, how much cash comes back, how fast, and how much genuine contribution remains?”

6. STOP / GO rules

Protect the ₹10–20K

🛑 STOP / MODIFY

Freight too high, weak repeat demand, poor margin, inconsistent quality, or stock moving too slowly.

🟡 SMALL REORDER

Some products sell but evidence is still limited. Reorder only proven items in small quantities.

🟢 SCALE

Repeat orders + healthy contribution + acceptable freight + controlled stock rotation. Then test retailers/Pune.

🎯 Pilot success is NOT “we sold everything”.

Success means we have enough evidence to answer four questions:

1. Which products do people repeat-buy?   2. What is the real landed cost?   3. What contribution remains after direct costs?   4. Can this repeat without putting large capital at risk?
